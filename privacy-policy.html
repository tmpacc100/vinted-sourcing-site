Privacy Policy

Cuucca · Effective date: October 15, 2026

Overview

Cuucca (“the app”) is a personal reselling and sourcing assistant for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: the app is built to be minimal. It has no shared or central server operated by us, it does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, it does not sell your data, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Optional local demo workspace

You can explicitly enable Demo Mode from the first-launch screen or Settings. It uses synthetic sample listings and illustrations, with sample preferences, saved items, decisions, resale calculations, and labels stored on your device. This workspace does not contact a backend, fetch the URL you enter, place orders, scrape listings, send notifications, or change your real backend credentials or data. Demo changes are separate from your configured backend. You can reset the demo workspace in Settings.

Your own backend server

The app communicates only with a backend server that you configure yourself. You enter a base URL and an API key in the app’s own Settings. There is no central service run by a third party that receives your data. All requests the app makes go to the server address you provide. If you do not configure a backend, the app has nothing to connect to.

Data stored on your device

The app stores data locally on your device to function:

  • A cache of listings and deal rankings (stored using SwiftData) so browsing is fast and works smoothly.
  • An outbox of your decisions and labels — for example your buy/skip choices, watchlist selections, and taste preferences — queued to be sent to your configured backend.
  • Your app settings, including the backend base URL and API key you entered.

This data stays on your device except where you choose to send it to your own backend (see below). You can remove it by deleting the app.

Data sent to your backend

Information you provide — your sourcing preferences, buy/skip decisions, watchlist actions, and taste labels — is sent only to the backend server you configured. Because that server is yours, you control how that data is stored and used on it. We do not receive, host, or have access to it.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, the app can send push notifications through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to alert you about new matching deals. Delivering these notifications involves a device token provided by Apple and used by your backend to reach your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not operate a central server that collects your data.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this URL.
