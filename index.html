Vinted Sourcing

Vinted Sourcing is a personal reselling and sourcing assistant for iOS. It ranks second-hand fashion “deals” by a combined taste-and-value score, lets you watchlist interesting items, and log your buy or skip decisions so your picks get sharper over time.

How it works

  • You connect the app to your own backend server (a base URL and API key you enter in Settings).
  • Ranked deals are cached locally on your device so browsing stays fast.
  • Optional push notifications alert you when new matching deals appear.
  • Your buy/skip decisions and taste labels are sent only to your own configured backend.
